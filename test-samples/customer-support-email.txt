Hi Product Team,

Our support team is drowning in tickets! We desperately need a better customer support portal. Here are our requirements based on feedback from 15 support agents:

MUST HAVE FEATURES:

1. Ticket Management System
Our agents need to:
- Create tickets from emails, chat, and phone calls
- Assign tickets to specific agents or teams
- Set priority levels (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
- Add internal notes that customers can't see
- Attach files and screenshots to tickets
- Merge duplicate tickets
- Link related tickets together

Current pain point: We're using a shared email inbox and losing track of who's working on what. Tickets are getting lost or handled by multiple people.

2. Knowledge Base Integration
- Search knowledge base articles while viewing tickets
- Suggest relevant articles to customers automatically
- Create new KB articles from resolved tickets
- Track which articles are most helpful
- Allow customers to rate article helpfulness

We answer the same questions 50+ times per day. A good KB could reduce ticket volume by 30%.

3. Customer Portal
Customers should be able to:
- Submit tickets without emailing us
- Track ticket status in real-time
- View their ticket history
- Upload attachments (screenshots, logs)
- Rate their support experience
- Search knowledge base before submitting ticket

4. Automation & Workflows
- Auto-assign tickets based on keywords or category
- Send automatic acknowledgment emails
- Escalate tickets that haven't been touched in 24 hours
- Auto-close tickets after 7 days of inactivity
- Trigger notifications for high-priority tickets
- SLA tracking with alerts

5. Reporting & Analytics
We need dashboards showing:
- Average response time
- Average resolution time
- Ticket volume by category
- Agent performance metrics
- Customer satisfaction scores
- SLA compliance rate
- Peak hours for ticket volume

PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS:
- Ticket list must load in under 1 second
- Search results in under 500ms
- Support 50 concurrent agents
- Handle 1000+ tickets per day
- 99.9% uptime (we can't afford downtime)

INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS:
- Must integrate with our CRM (Salesforce)
- Email integration (support@techcorp.com)
- Slack notifications for urgent tickets
- Zapier integration for custom workflows

SECURITY REQUIREMENTS:
- Role-based access (Admin, Manager, Agent, Customer)
- Audit log of all ticket changes
- Data encryption at rest and in transit
- GDPR compliance for customer data
- Secure file uploads (virus scanning)

TIMELINE:
We need this ASAP! Our current system is costing us customers. Can we have an MVP in 8 weeks?

Budget: $150,000 approved

Let me know if you need more details!

Jennifer Martinez
Customer Support Manager

- AWS S3 for file storage

3. Infrastructure:
- AWS hosting with auto-scaling
- CloudFront CDN for static assets
- Load balancer for high availability
- Automated backups every 6 hours
- Multi-region deployment for reliability

- Extract attachments from emails
- Handle email threading
- Support email templates
- Track email delivery status
