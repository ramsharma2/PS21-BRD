PROJECT: TeleMed - Healthcare Telemedicine Platform

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
TeleMed is a comprehensive telemedicine platform designed to connect patients with healthcare providers through secure video consultations, digital prescriptions, and health record management. The platform aims to improve healthcare accessibility, reduce wait times, and provide quality care remotely.

BUSINESS GOALS:
- Enable 10,000 virtual consultations per month within first 6 months
- Reduce average patient wait time from 2 weeks to 24 hours
- Achieve 95% patient satisfaction rating
- Ensure HIPAA compliance for all patient data handling
- Generate $2M in revenue within first year

STAKEHOLDER ANALYSIS:

Dr. Emily Rodriguez (Chief Medical Officer):
"Patient safety is paramount. We need real-time vital signs monitoring integration with IoT devices. The system must alert providers if patient vitals are outside normal ranges during consultations."

"Prescription management must include drug interaction checking and allergy alerts. We can't risk prescribing medications that could harm patients."

"Provider credentials must be verified and licenses must be tracked with expiration alerts. We need to ensure only licensed professionals can provide care."

"The platform must support 1,000 concurrent video sessions without quality degradation. Video quality should automatically adjust based on bandwidth."

"We need 99.99% uptime - this is healthcare, people's lives depend on access to care. Downtime is not acceptable."

Rachel Kim (Patient Advocate):
"The interface must be simple enough for elderly patients to use. My 75-year-old mother should be able to join a consultation without help."

"Patients need to access their complete medical history, test results, and prescriptions in one place. No more calling different offices for records."

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

Patient Registration & Profile:
- Patients must register with email, phone number, and basic demographics
- Complete medical history intake form (allergies, medications, conditions)
- Insurance information capture and verification
- Emergency contact information
- Profile photo upload for identification
- Multi-factor authentication for account security

Provider Registration & Credentialing:
- Healthcare provider registration with license verification
- Medical specialty and sub-specialty selection
- Board certification documentation upload
- Malpractice insurance verification
- Background check integration
- Availability calendar management
- Consultation fee setting per appointment type

Appointment Scheduling:
- Real-time provider availability display
- Filter providers by specialty, language, rating, availability
- Appointment booking with instant confirmation
- Automated reminder notifications (email, SMS, push)
- Appointment rescheduling up to 2 hours before start time
- Cancellation with refund policy enforcement
- Waitlist functionality for fully booked providers

Video Consultation:
- HD video calling with screen sharing capability
- Real-time chat during consultation
- Virtual waiting room with queue management
- Consultation recording with patient consent
- Vital signs display integration (heart rate, blood pressure, temperature)
- Digital whiteboard for explanations and diagrams
- File sharing for test results and images
- Automatic session timeout after 60 minutes

Electronic Health Records (EHR):
- Comprehensive patient health record storage
- Lab results and imaging reports upload
- Medication list with dosage and frequency
- Allergy and adverse reaction tracking
- Immunization history
- Family medical history
- Visit notes and diagnosis documentation
- Export records in standard formats (HL7, FHIR)

E-Prescribing:
- Digital prescription creation and transmission to pharmacies
- Drug interaction checking against patient medications
- Allergy alert system
- Controlled substance prescribing with DEA verification
- Prescription history tracking
- Refill request management
- Pharmacy selection and routing

Billing & Payments:
- Insurance eligibility verification in real-time
- Co-pay calculation and collection
- Multiple payment methods (credit card, HSA/FSA, insurance)
- Invoice generation and email delivery
- Payment history and receipt access
- Refund processing for cancelled appointments
- Insurance claim submission automation

Notifications & Reminders:
- Appointment reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before
- Medication reminders based on prescription schedule
- Lab result availability notifications
- Provider message notifications
- System maintenance alerts
- Health tips and wellness content

Admin Dashboard:
- User management (patients, providers, staff)
- Appointment monitoring and analytics
- Revenue reporting and financial analytics
- Compliance audit logs
- System health monitoring
- Provider performance metrics
- Patient satisfaction surveys

NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

Security & Compliance:
- HIPAA compliance for all PHI (Protected Health Information)
- End-to-end encryption for video consultations (AES-256)
- Data encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Role-based access control (RBAC) with principle of least privilege
- Audit logging of all PHI access and modifications
- Automatic session timeout after 15 minutes of inactivity
- Password complexity requirements (12+ characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols)
- Two-factor authentication mandatory for providers
- Regular security penetration testing (quarterly)
- GDPR compliance for international patients
- SOC 2 Type II certification
- Business Associate Agreements (BAA) with all vendors

Performance:
- Video consultation latency under 150ms
- Page load time under 1.5 seconds
- Support 1,000 concurrent video sessions
- Database query response time under 50ms
- API response time under 100ms for 99% of requests
- Image and document upload under 5 seconds for 10MB files
- Search results display within 500ms
- Real-time notification delivery within 2 seconds

Reliability & Availability:
- 99.99% uptime SLA (maximum 52 minutes downtime per year)
- Automated failover to backup systems within 30 seconds
- Real-time data replication across multiple data centers
- Automated backup every 4 hours with 90-day retention
- Disaster recovery with 1-hour RTO and 15-minute RPO
- Load balancing across multiple servers
- Database clustering with automatic failover
- Health check monitoring every 30 seconds

Scalability:
- Horizontal scaling to support 100,000 registered users
- Auto-scaling based on concurrent user load
- CDN integration for static content delivery
- Database sharding for large data volumes
- Microservices architecture for independent component scaling
- Message queue for asynchronous processing
- Caching layer (Redis) for frequently accessed data

Usability & Accessibility:
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance
- Mobile-responsive design (iOS and Android browsers)
- Support for screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver)
- Keyboard navigation for all functionality
- High contrast mode for visually impaired users
- Font size adjustment (100% to 200%)
- Multi-language support (English, Spanish, Mandarin)

- Intuitive UI requiring no training for patients

Interoperability:
- HL7 FHIR API for EHR integration
- Integration with major pharmacy networks (Surescripts)
- Lab result integration (HL7 v2.x)
- Insurance eligibility verification (X12 270/271)

- Must use HIPAA-compliant cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP)
- Must support IPv6 for future-proofing

- Third-party services (payment gateways, SMS providers) maintain 99.9% uptime
