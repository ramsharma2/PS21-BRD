Team,

After discussing with stakeholders, here's the decision:

PHASE 1 OFFLINE MODE (SIMPLIFIED):
- Read-only access to previously viewed products
- Cache last 50 viewed products
- Cache user's cart and wishlist
- Show "offline" indicator in UI
- No write operations while offline
- Sync automatically when online

This reduces complexity significantly and keeps us on timeline.

PHASE 2 OFFLINE MODE (FULL):
- Add to cart while offline
- Update profile while offline
- Conflict resolution
- Full sync capability

Everyone good with this approach?
